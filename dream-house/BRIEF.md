# Dream House — Design Brief (working draft)

Living reference: **191 Webster St** (in this repo) is the "what does a real space feel like" model.
This brief collects what we want in the new house. Sizes below are first-pass targets for the 3D model,
using the designer clearances the layout audit checks (36" walkways, 42" kitchen aisle, 30" around beds).

## Household
- Now: 2 people. In ~10 years: 5. Frequent guests; dogs and cats.
- Hosting: often, 10+ people.
- Works from home: one office with a door.

## Site & budget
- Massachusetts, ideally near the water. No land yet.
- Budget: ~$750k (land + house). ⚠ See "Budget reality check" below.

## Form & style
- 2 stories + full basement, attic storage.
- Exterior: coastal Cape (shingle/clapboard, steep roof, dormers).
- Interior: cozy and warm, but bright and airy. Some exposed wood beams.
- Defined rooms (not fully open); interior windows/openings between kitchen and dining or living are welcome.
- Accessible: wide halls and doors, a first-floor bedroom suite for guests / aging in place.

## Budget reality check (rough, verify with local builders and agents)
Ballpark only. Prices change, and this is not a quote.
- New custom construction in MA has recently run roughly **$350–500+ per sq ft**. At ~3,800 sq ft
  that's well over $1.3M before land.
- Waterfront or near-water land in coastal MA is often **$300k to $1M+**.
- So everything on this list, new, near the water, is likely **~2–3× the $750k budget**. The levers:
  1. **Buy an existing Cape/Colonial and renovate/add on** — often the best value near the coast.
  2. **Shrink the program** (e.g. ~2,600–2,800 sq ft: 4 bed / 2½ bath, theme room + gym share the basement).
  3. **Phase it**: build the core now, finish the basement, sunroom, deck, shed later.
  4. **"Near" the water** (a town or two inland, or a water view rather than frontage) instead of on it.
  5. A **semi-custom/modular** builder for the shell, custom finishes inside.
